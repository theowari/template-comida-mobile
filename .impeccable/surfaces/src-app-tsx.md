# Storefront (Seu negócio)

Scope: the whole ordering app (home, menu, item sheet, bag, checkout, orders, offers, profile). Visitor mode: Operate. Build path: code-led (Operate surface; no image generation available).

Audience and job: hungry Brazilian customer on a phone who wants to find, customize and order food fast, using conventions already learned in delivery apps.

Constraints: visual composition pinned by the reference image in docs/; palette and Inter pinned by the brief; brand "Seu negócio"; no emojis; Unsplash photos; demo data labeled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A storefront that feels already learned. Delivery-app ordering grammar (search on top, category rail, priced cards with a red add button, floating bag bar, item sheet with priced options, stepped checkout) inside the reference's warm pizzeria world. It refuses the restaurant landing page (hero, about, gallery, contact).

OWN-WORLD: Flour-white ground #F7F3EF; white rounded tiles and cards with soft warm offset shadows; pizzeria red #D71935 only for action, selection and price-critical state; near-black oven banner #17110F; condensed uppercase Barlow Condensed for banner headlines and the wordmark, Inter for everything operable; food photos in rounded frames.

STORY: The visitor sees the brand, delivery time and fee, finds a pizza in seconds, customizes it with every price visible, adds it, watches the bag bar update, checks out in four short steps, and always knows what is a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390px: header row (menu tile, greeting over logo and tagline, bell tile, bag tile with count); search pill with filter tile; one line of delivery facts; dark hero banner with badge, two-line condensed headline (white, then red), short copy and a red pill CTA, photo filling the right; the top of the category rail. The bag stays at the top right; the bottom nav is fixed with a raised red center.

FORM: Brief-pinned reference composition (position 1 of 1; no roll). Signature move: the floating bag bar that rises above the bottom nav with item count and running total, paired with the live price on the item sheet's add button.
